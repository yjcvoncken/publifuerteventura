What this site stores

PubliFuerteventura uses essential browser storage to remember your privacy preference. Security cookies may also be set when you sign into the administration area or submit a protected form.

Optional analytics

If you consent, we record the page path, language, time and a one-way hashed anonymous session identifier. We do not store your IP address or raw user-agent for analytics, and no third-party analytics provider receives this information. The information is used to understand which pages are useful and improve the website.

Managing your choice

Use “Cookie settings” in the footer to reopen the consent panel at any time. Choosing “Essential only” stops future analytics collection. You can also clear cookies, local storage and session storage through your browser. Rejecting analytics does not prevent access to the website.

Questions may be sent to privacy@publifuerteventura.com.
