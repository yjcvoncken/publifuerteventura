Is the price monthly or annual?+

All plans are billed annually. Basic costs €100, Advanced costs €250 and Custom starts from €300 per year.

Can businesses buy positive reviews?+

No. Custom members pay for access to the review-credit programme, not for a rating. Credits can encourage verified customers to share an honest experience, whether positive or negative.

Does every business get accepted?+

No. We review each application to keep the platform relevant, useful and trustworthy.

What happens after I apply?+

We review your business, contact you with the plan details and confirm everything before requesting payment.
